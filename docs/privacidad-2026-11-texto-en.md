# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026 · Version 2026-11_

## 1. Who we are

3G Intelligence is an artificial intelligence consultancy offering diagnostics and automation solutions for businesses. Contact: chat@3gi.cl

3G Intelligence is the trade name of 3GI SpA, tax ID (RUT) 78.388.535-2, domiciled in Viña del Mar, Chile, represented by Gonzalo Gompertz Geldres. 3GI SpA is the controller of your personal data. To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, write to chat@3gi.cl.

## 2. Data we collect

- Phone number when contacting via WhatsApp
- Messages sent to the WhatsApp bot
- Business information entered in the Test ERA form (name, sector, size, processes)
- Information you or your company provide in the Radar Preparation questionnaire: how your business works (team, tools, processes, types of data you handle, security measures, sales channels, regulatory compliance and expectations), together with your name and contact email. We do not ask for personal data about your customers.
- Traffic analytics cookies (Google Analytics and PostHog) and advertising measurement cookies (LinkedIn) — see section 8
- A record of the steps of your journey in the ERA Test (page, step reached and campaign tags from the address; only if you accept cookies is your full journey followed) — see section 8
## 3. How we use your data

- To qualify and process Test ERA report requests
- To follow up commercially with the prospect
- To prepare and run your Radar and, if you request it, the follow-up work proposal (pre-contractual steps at your request)
- To generate the Radar report, for which part of your answers is processed with artificial intelligence models
- To generate internal diagnostic reports
- To analyze and improve the ERA Test journey and measure the results of our campaigns
## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or transfer data to third parties. We use the following service providers under their own privacy policies:

- **Anthropic** — natural language processing (Claude AI)
- **OpenAI** — embeddings generation for semantic search
- **Meta** — WhatsApp Business messaging channel
- **Supabase** — data storage (United States)
- **Hetzner** — infrastructure hosting (Germany)
- **Cloudflare** — hosting and delivery of the site and of the service that receives the questionnaire
- **Telegram** — internal alerts to our team (company name and number of answers, never their content)
- **Google** — website traffic analytics (Google Analytics), only if you accept analytics cookies (see section 8)
- **PostHog** — product analytics and recordings of the journey on the site, only if you accept cookies (see section 8)
- **LinkedIn** — advertising conversion measurement (LinkedIn Insight Tag), only if you accept cookies (see section 8)
**Where your data is processed.** 3G Intelligence's automation infrastructure and application servers are in Germany (Hetzner), and the database where your answers are stored is in the United States (Supabase). Cloudflare delivers the site from its global network. The other providers process data in the countries where their services operate, including the United States, under their own policies. If we transfer your data outside Chile, we do so only to these providers and only to provide the service described in this policy. You can ask us for more information at chat@3gi.cl.

## 5. Data retention

Data is retained for the duration of the business relationship or until the user requests deletion. If no work is contracted with us, questionnaire answers and other preparation data are deleted or anonymized no later than 12 months after the last contact. The questionnaire link expires after 14 days.

## 6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, objection, and portability of your data at any time. You also have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing of your data that significantly affect you, to request an explanation of such decisions, and to request human intervention or review. Write to us at chat@3gi.cl.

From 1 December 2026 you may also request the temporary suspension of the processing of your data while a request for correction, deletion or objection is being resolved. The suspension does not affect the storage of your data.

From 1 December 2026, when Law 21,719 comes into force, if we do not answer your request or we reject it, you may also lodge a complaint with the Chilean Personal Data Protection Agency (Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales).

## 7. Security

Data is stored on secure servers with restricted access. No passwords or financial information are shared through our channels.

The Radar Preparation questionnaire opens only with a unique, non-transferable link that expires and that we can revoke. The connection is encrypted, access to the answers is restricted and that page uses no analytics tools or cookies.

## 8. Cookies

With your consent we use three third-party tools: Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager) to understand how this site is used; PostHog to analyze the ERA Test journey (page views, clicks, completed steps and recordings of your browsing on the site); and, if you arrive from a LinkedIn ad, the LinkedIn Insight Tag to measure how many people complete the ERA Test after seeing the ad. They are only loaded if you accept them in the notice shown on your first visit: if you reject them, none of them is loaded.

Even if you reject cookies, the ERA Test and the resources pages record the steps of your journey (for example, which test question you are on and the page) using a random identifier that exists only in the memory of the page: it is not stored in your browser and it is lost when you change page or close it. If you arrived from an ad, we also record the campaign tags in the address (for example, the name of the campaign and of the ad), without storing them in your browser and only tied to the steps on that page. With your consent, that identifier is stored in your browser while the tab stays open, and those parameters are also recorded throughout your journey, so we can follow it in full. In both cases the record does not include your name, email or company data, and it is stored only on our own servers (Supabase). We also count, without identifying you, how many people accept or reject cookies. We use it to learn where people drop out of the test and improve it.

You can change your cookie decision at any time via the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of this page.
